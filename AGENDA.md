## Timeline

| Time | Title | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 10:00 – 10:15 | Arrival & Coffee | Check-in, coffee and light refreshments, informal networking. Background slide deck looping with agenda, Wi-Fi, tools, and prompts. |

| 2:45 – 3:15 | Prepare Presentations | Teams prepare a tight 3-minute structure: Problem (1 min), What we built (1 min), Live demo (1 min). |
| 3:15 – 4:00 | Presentations ⭐ | 3 minutes per team plus short Q&A (2 min max per team). |
| 4:00 – 4:30 | Closing | Group reflection: What was a win? What surprised you? What will you implement next week? Close with next steps and follow-up support options. |
