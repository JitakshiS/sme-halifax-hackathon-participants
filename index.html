Common questions

Here's what everyone asks before signing up.

Do I really not need to know how to code? +

Really. The AI tools we use (Lovable, Claude Code, v0) generate code for you. You describe what you want in plain English. The mentors handle everything technical. If you can write an email, you can build at this hackathon.

What if I don't know what to build? +

That's what the Problem Clarity Sprint is for. In the first 45 minutes, mentors help you identify the exact business problem worth solving and scope it for the day. Many participants arrive without a clue and leave with a working tool.

Is this going to be over my head? +

This event is designed for business owners, not developers. The mentors, the tools, and the pace are all calibrated for people who've never built software before. If it feels hard, you're doing it wrong — flag a mentor.

What do I actually leave with? +

A working prototype you can demo, share, and continue building. Plus $200+ in free AI tool credits so you can keep going after the event. And a certificate, if that matters to you.

Can I come alone or do I need a team? +

Both work. Come solo ($150 for 1–2 people) or bring your team ($250 for 3–5 people). Solo participants often pair up on the day. Either way, mentors are with you the whole time.

Is $150–250 worth it? +

You get a full day of expert mentorship, $200+ in AI tool credits, breakfast, lunch, prizes, and a working prototype. Most people spend more than that on a single consulting call that gives them a PDF. This gives you a product.
